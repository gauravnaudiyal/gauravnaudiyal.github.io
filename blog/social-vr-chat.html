Social VR Chat

An XR project with three different themed environments in VRChat, built for multiplayer communication.

What it is

This was a group project where we designed three separate VR spaces meant for people to hang out and talk in. Each theme had a different vibe and layout, and we thought a lot about how people move and interact when they're not alone in a virtual space.

What I worked on

  • Helped design and build out two of the three themed environments
  • Worked on player interaction and how avatars communicate with each other
  • Tested the spaces with classmates to see what felt natural versus awkward

What I learned

Designing for social VR is different from designing for solo VR. Things like personal space and how avatars face each other during conversation matter a lot more than I expected going in.
