Distributed Systems

A group coursework project under the team name Tribunal of Human Expansion, focused on system design.

What it is

This was our Distributed Systems module project. We had to design and build a system that could handle multiple nodes talking to each other reliably, thinking through trade offs like consistency versus speed.

What I worked on

  • Helped design the overall system architecture with the team
  • Worked on how nodes communicated and handled failure cases
  • Split coding and testing tasks with teammates and reviewed each other's work

What I learned

Distributed systems fail in ways you don't expect until you actually test them. A lot of the learning came from watching things break and figuring out why.
