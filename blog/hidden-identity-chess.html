Hidden Identity Chess

A real-time, imperfect-information chess variant played over WebSockets, featuring a custom deduction bot and zero-knowledge state security.

What it is

I built a multiplayer chess game where you can't see the identity of your opponent's pieces until they are captured or make a capture. It's played on a custom 5x7 grid. The main challenge was ensuring the client couldn't cheat by inspecting network payloads.

What I worked on

  • Architected a real-time authoritative backend using Node.js and Socket.io.
  • Implemented zero-knowledge state isolation to actively redact hidden opponent data before broadcasting payloads, preventing client-side network snooping.
  • Developed a solo-play deduction bot and custom game engine to calculate board states and validate legal moves.
