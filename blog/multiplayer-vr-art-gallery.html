Multiplayer VR Art Gallery

A shared VR gallery space where multiple people can walk around, look at art, and talk at the same time.

What it is

This was a group project called Project X, built with a team under the name World Domination Plan. The idea was simple: put people together in a virtual gallery and see how they explore it and interact with each other while doing it.

What I worked on

  • Worked on networking so multiple users could be in the same scene at once
  • Helped build out navigation and how users move through the gallery space
  • Tested with the team to catch bugs around desync and player position

What I learned

Multiplayer VR adds a whole layer of complexity that solo VR projects don't have. Keeping everyone's position and actions in sync without lag was harder than I expected.
